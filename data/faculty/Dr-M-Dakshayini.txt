About:
Dr. M Dakshayini, holding M.E degree with FCD, awarded Ph.D in the area of computer networks in 2010. Presently she is working as Professor and Head in the Department of Information Science and engineering, B M S College of Engineering[BMSCE], Bengaluru, Karnataka, India.
She has 26 years of teaching experience, 5 research candidates have been awarded with Ph.D degree under her guidance and presently guiding 1 research scholar towards research and Ph.D. in the area of Computer communications, Algorithms, Cloud computing, Ad-hoc Networks, IoT and Data Sciences. She has delivered expert lectures at various Seminars/Workshops. She is BOS/BOE member for various Institutions. She is a NBA Evaluator appointed from NBA, India.
She has more than 50 International peer reviewed Journal Publications, 26 International conference and 5 national conference Publications to her credit. She has published a patent for the research work “Co-operative Proxy Servers Architecture for Video on Demand and Webservers using Stochastic Model based Client Request Management Approaches to achieve reduced Client Waiting Time Reference number [3390/CHE/2010A].
