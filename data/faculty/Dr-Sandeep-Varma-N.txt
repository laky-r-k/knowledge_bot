About:
Dr. Sandeep is currently working as Associate Professor in the Department of Machine Learning, B.M.S. College of Engineering.   He received his B. Tech degree in Information Technology from JNTU Hyderabad, Telangana, India in 2007. He received his M. Tech from Andhra University in 2009 and his Ph.D. in Computer Science and Systems Engineering from Andhra University in 2015. He also worked as JRF & SRF Fellowships from 2010 to 2013, Andhra University under DST SERB Project. Dr. Sandeep is a very active researcher and currently executing R&D sponsored projects of worth 52.17 Lakhs from Industry, Ministry of Education, Govt. of India & ARTPARK, IISc, in the fields of Neural Networks, Deep Learning and IOT.  His research interest include Formal Verification Techniques for security protocols, Data Engineering, Software Engineering and AI. He is a also Chapter Advisor to BMSCE IEEE Computer Society Chapter, Bangalore Section.
